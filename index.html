How the stations work

Each volunteer runs one station: a treat, a fun game, an activity, or a craft. The kids rotate in groups, so plan for 7 kids to visit your station 4 times, 10 minutes each round.

Bring your supplies and materials ready to go. Need an idea? Reach out to Kelsie and we'll brainstorm.

Treat rules

  • Store bought food only (no homemade)
